## Testing details

Headless Chromium using SwiftShader on the sandbox tested development build: opening, movement, mouse drag, jump, map opening, a seeded near-waystone interaction and persistence after reload; touch joystick and zoom were tested with emulated touch events. The production build opened, saved and displayed the map in headless Chromium in the prior milestone. The current quest loop was completed in the development build via scripted keyboard traversal from the normal spawn, including speaking to the guide, gathering, crafting, restoring and reloading. Emulated-touch portrait checks opened settings, bag and map without horizontal overflow. Rain and graphics settings persisted after reload; a production build opened and changed weather. After moving the project to the root, production browser smoke-tested movement and map, and the streamed dev build completed the quest with keyboard traversal. An immediate reload exposed an asynchronous save race; the HUD now says when a save is pending and a best-effort beforeunload warning protects pending writes. A browser test waited for the saved indicator and verified restoration; mobile browsers may ignore unload warnings. The Echo Grove was reached from the normal spawn by keyboard, solved, and its XP/skill persisted after reload. Emulated multi-touch exercised simultaneous joystick and sprint; portrait journal opened without horizontal overflow. Browser instrumentation showed zero audio contexts before the start gesture and restored mute/volume after reload. Production build opened the journal. In the later milestone the river shader compiled without console errors and a swimmer crossed to the bank from a seeded position; berry gathering, camp cooking and meal persistence were exercised by keyboard traversal from spawn. The guardian was exercised with seeded player position and enemy health: attack, damage, defeat and persistence; emulated touch dodge reduced stamina. A seeded landmark visit awarded XP and persisted in the journal after reload. After vegetation streaming, a browser traversal gathered and crafted the quest items, reached the waystone and read the Echo Grove inscription; chunk counts agreed and no console errors were observed. Real Rapier vegetation collider removal is covered by a Node test. No page errors or console errors were observed in those smoke runs. Software rendering is not a performance test and does not establish real-device compatibility.

Next milestone: physical Android/iOS and desktop QA, improve camera collision and input/accessibility, split remaining runtime responsibilities and measure performance. Profile experimental chunk streaming on actual devices, add background generation, LOD and asset disposal before expanding terrain. Do not label Phase 1 complete until its acceptance criteria are tested.
